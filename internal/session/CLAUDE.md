**Session setup** (internal/session/setupexecutor.go): setup steps run as a dependency graph. A `Background` step (`install_deps`) runs outside it: it doesn't hold back `apply_claude`, `setup_mux` or the session going `active`, its step stays `running` until it finishes, and a failure is appended to the active session's `status_error` (`AppendStatusError`). A follower (`RunSetupInForeground`) runs every step in the graph, so the session goes active only after the install. A daemon restart mid-install leaves the step `running`.
